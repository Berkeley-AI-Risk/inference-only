# HWIC research progress — November 3, 2026

The fresh mapped-ROM16 query-projection regression passed all four normal seeds:
68,544 checked output rows across 264 vector replays. The deliberately corrupted
INV/MUX circuit failed at the intended first public arithmetic check, case 0,
row 0, with a genuine native exit of 1. This completes the pending block-level
regression from the [October 29 progress note](PROGRESS-2026-10-29.md).

The [October 27 SimpleStories RTL checkpoint](simplestories-2026-10-27/README.md)
remains the published executable baseline. These new results cover a mapped
query-projection block and its finite corpus. They do not qualify the newer
spatial full-model candidate or establish ASIC clock frequency, area, power,
token rate or manufacturing readiness.

## Recorded results and reusable tooling

The [compact result record](evidence/rom16-2026-11-03/results.json) contains selected
log excerpts, original full-log hashes, native exit codes, resource measurements,
artifact hashes and independent-review hashes. Four positive seeds were 1, 17,
20261028 and 2147483646; each checked 17,136 rows and 66 vectors, including ordered
packets, stalls, bubbles, held outputs, CLEAR and fault resets. Generation, build
and replay comprised nine native stages. All observed owned process groups
drained without a resource-guard failure.

One normal build's native Make process succeeded, while its original controller
failed because of a path-construction bug in the artifact checker. A corrected
file-only checker subsequently verified the existing build without rerunning it.
The original controller failure remains part of the recorded result.

The accompanying helpers support raw-source hash verification, bounded child
ownership, and archive/object byte comparison. They are reusable components,
not a complete public reproduction package for the mapped circuit. Model assets,
mapped netlists, generated compiler trees and process/toolchain libraries remain
outside this update. To check the archived excerpts and helper tests offline:

~~~sh
python3 -I -S -B research/hwic/tools/check_recorded_rom16.py
python3 -I -S -B research/hwic/tools/test_checkpoint_tools.py
~~~

The nine native stages consumed 2,642.742133 measured CPU seconds in total.
Per-stage wall times are recorded separately because the four normal replays ran
in parallel. Source preparation and review costs are separate. New paid-service
charges were $0; provider token counts, provider billing, local energy costs and
total research cost were unavailable and remain null.

## Next milestones

The next target is the integrated SimpleStories spatial candidate's complete
seven-case numerical regression: 53 token outputs and 2,605,355 selected tensor
and logit comparisons, followed by circuit-fault sensitivity checks. Its current
source and lint checks passed; this complete-model run remains pending.

After numerical validation, compare implementations through routing, extracted
timing and representative-activity power with a credible physical-memory plan.
ROM/SRAM geometry and LVS, whole-chip clocks and reset, DRC/LVS, PVT analysis and
manufacturing-test design remain open. SimpleStories remains the first ASIC
target; reusable AWIC/HWIC components and spatial parallelism remain priorities.

The research draws on Yang Liu et al., [*Hardwired-Neurons Language Processing
Units as General-Purpose Cognitive Substrates*,
arXiv:2508.16151v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16151v2). The current baseline uses
fixed ROM feeding shared arithmetic adapted from the AWIC token core. HNLPU's
custom physical weight-routing design has not been reproduced, and no comparative
silicon speed or area advantage has been established.
