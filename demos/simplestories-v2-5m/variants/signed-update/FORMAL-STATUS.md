# S-AWIC formal scope

The whole-machine objective is that every public command stream implements APPEND, STEP with the admitted fixed-architecture model, and CLEAR, or fails closed. Signed admission must authorize the model before use. That theorem remains open.

The [Tang formal status](tang/FORMAL-STATUS.md) describes two full-geometry page/accounting and compressor-ownership proofs and five stream-assembly equivalence configurations. Their source snapshots, assumptions, queries and raw engine results are packaged separately from physical evidence. The optional [periodic-reauthorization control proof](../home-authorization/formal/authorization-control/README.md) adds twelve conditional control jobs for that variant.

These component results support the authorization and functional-correctness argument. They do not prove the complete numerical model, SHA/LMS security, hostile-memory tolerance, physical security or a residual-training bound. Signature-only updates implement no delay, lifetime cap or monotonic-version policy.
